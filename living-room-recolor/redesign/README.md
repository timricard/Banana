# Living Room — Affordable Redesign (2 angles)

Built on the Pashmina / Alabaster recolor. This is a **budget refresh** — no renovation,
no new sofas. It only **rearranges existing furniture, removes one couch, adds the two
wood-legged chairs** from the TV room, and **layers the lighting**.

## The problem
The room has three bulky matching gray sofas crowded in a U around the coffee table.
It reads heavy, blocks circulation, and (from the fireplace angle) the loveseat sits with
its back to the hearth — hiding the room's best focal point.

## The moves (all low/no cost)
1. **Subtract a couch.** Drop from three gray sofas to two — instantly more floor and flow.
2. **Bring in the two wood-legged chairs** (gray cushion + woven leather sling) you already
   own. Their open walnut frames add warmth and lightness where a third solid couch added bulk,
   and the wood ties to the coffee table, credenza, brass, and the warm Pashmina walls.
3. **Reorient to the fireplace.** In the hearth view, seating now faces the fireplace instead
   of turning its back to it, with the coffee table centering a real conversation group.
4. **Layer the lighting** (the highest-impact cheap change): keep the tripod floor lamp, add a
   warm table lamp on the credenza + a slim floor lamp by the reading chairs, a small picture
   light over the mantel art, and warm 2700K bulbs throughout so the greige walls glow.
