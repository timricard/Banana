# Dual-purpose TV + Living layout

Test of placing a 65" TV where the record player is (above the walnut mid-century credenza on
the wall by the arch), while keeping the room good for both TV watching and everyday living.
Built on the redesigned Living4b (Pashmina walls, the two wood-legged chairs, layered lighting).

- The credenza stays as the media console (record player + records kept, slim soundbar added).
- Two seating options rendered:
  - `tv_layout_A.png` — one sofa facing the TV wall, wood chairs + second sofa completing a
    conversation circle; cocktail art relocated so the TV is the focal point.
  - `tv_layout_B.png` — L-shaped sofas with the cocktail art kept beside the TV.
- `tv_before_after.jpg` — record-player wall vs. the TV layout.

## Does it work for both?
Yes, with minor tuning. The wall comfortably fits a 65" above the credenza and the ~8-10 ft
viewing distance suits that size. The two wood chairs and the left sofa get the best sightlines;
the foreground sofa faces away, so a swivel chair or angling that sofa improves it. TV isn't
opposite a large window, so daytime glare should be manageable. The credenza sits right next to
the archway, so the TV is slightly off-center — a swivel mount helps.
