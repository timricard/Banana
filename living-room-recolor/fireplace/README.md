# Fireplace Redesign — 3 affordable options

The firebox surround is distressed red brick with old peeling paint, and the hearth tile
is worn — both fight the clean Alabaster mantel. All three options keep the existing white
mantel, the black wood-stove insert, floors, windows, and plants; they only refinish the
**brick** and **hearth**. Ranked cheapest/easiest first.

1. **Limewash** (`limewash.png`) — a soft warm whitewash over the brick; texture still reads
   through. Off-white handmade-look tile hearth. Brightest and freshest; classic DIY limewash
   (~$50-80 in materials). Best all-rounder with the Pashmina/Alabaster palette.
2. **Solid Alabaster** (`solid.png`) — brick painted the same warm white as the mantel for a
   seamless, quiet monochrome. Warm greige honed-stone hearth. Most modern/minimal. Masonry
   paint + primer, a weekend job.
3. **German smear / mortar wash** (`smear.png`) — soft white mortar dragged over the brick so
   warm natural red-brown still shows through; dark soapstone hearth. Coziest, most character.

`options_grid.jpg` shows all three next to the current state. Renders are directional — the
exact whitewash opacity and hearth material can be dialed in.
