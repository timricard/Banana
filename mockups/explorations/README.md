# Kitchen mockup explorations

Color/finish studies for the 256 Talbot kitchen. The chosen design lives one
level up as `mockups/kitchen_rosemary.png` (galley "View from Family") and
`mockups/kitchen_rosemary_view2.png` (range-wall angle): **Rosemary SW 6187
lower cabinets, Alabaster SW 7008 tone-on-tone walls/trim/uppers**, Carrara
marble, white subway tile, aged-brass, white oak floors, panel-front
dishwasher.

Naming: `kitchen_<green>_<view>_<wallTreatment>_<uppers>.png`
- view: `galley` (from the family room) or `view2` (range wall)
- greens: Rosemary (warm olive-sage) vs Pewter (deep gray-green, SW 6208)
- wall treatments: cream (SW Creamy 7012), pashmina (BM AF-100 greige),
  `*_match` (tone-on-tone walls+trim+uppers)
- uppers: White Dove (OC-17) vs Alabaster (SW 7008)
