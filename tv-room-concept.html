Warm Mid-Century TV Room

A warm mid-century room built around a rust velvet sofa as the single bold piece, grounded by a vintage-pattern rug and walnut furniture already in the room. A quieter, warm small-scale botanical wallpaper on the TV wall and natural white oak floors tie the space to the Dried Thyme and brass kitchen next door.

Floor plan

Drawn to scale at 1" = 4 px. Grid squares are 12" (1 ft). Dashed arcs show door swings; the dashed circle is the ceiling fixture; rust dimensions are clearances.

  • Sofa to chest: 18"Comfortable reach to the tabletop, enough knee room in front of the 46" deep seat.
  • Rug to wallsWall A 38", wall B 39", wall C 12", wall D 12". The rug lands centered in the room and the sofa front legs sit about 5" onto it.
  • Kitchen to French doors69" clear between chest and console; both door leaves swing a full 90 degrees clear of the lamp and lounge chair, leaving the full 60" opening.
  • Sofa ends18" from wall C to the sofa arm (floor lamp tucks in front of the left sidelight) and 18" from wall D (monstera in the corner).
